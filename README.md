# Rugga 27 — Mobile Rugby Union Management Simulation

Rugga 27 is a 2D rugby union management game built for mobile and desktop, inspired by classic sports management depth with an accessible, child-friendly user experience.

## Key Features

1. **Rugga 27 Brand Identity**: Original rugby-inspired visual design, featuring deep forest green navigation, fresh grass green accents, warm parchment backgrounds, club colors, and stadium matchday atmosphere.
2. **Strict Rugby Positional Rules**: True-to-life separation of Forwards (1–8) and Backs (9–15). Ordinary forwards cannot play in the backline, and backs cannot pack down in the scrum.
3. **The Hybrid Player Mechanic**: Modeled on real rugby versatility (e.g. Ben Earl, André Esterhuizen, Levani Botia, Kwagga Smith). Players with the researched `Hybrid` attribute can legally cover approved cross-unit positions with realistic position-specific ratings.
4. **Separate 2025–26 & 2026–27 Squad Snapshots**: Official club rosters for both seasons with confirmed signings, departures, and transparent verification sources.
5. **Accurate Player Ratings**: Base ability is kept distinct from temporary Form and Fitness. Ratings are calculated specifically for each position using weighted technical attributes.
6. **Polished Substitution Engine**: Pre-match and in-match substitutions with World Rugby Law 3 front-row safety checks, max 8 replacements, position compatibility warnings, and "Best Sub" recommendations.
7. **Child-Friendly & Accessible**: Simple Mode toggle with auto-selection, large touch-friendly buttons, clear plain English labels, and a built-in Rugby School glossary explaining scrums, rucks, lineouts, bonus points, and hybrid versatility.
8. **Authentic URC Rivalries & Fan Satisfaction**: Full coverage of genuine derbies (North-South Derby: Stormers vs Bulls, Jukskei Derby: Lions vs Bulls, Irish Interprovs: Leinster vs Munster, 1872 Cup: Glasgow vs Edinburgh, Italian Derby: Benetton vs Zebre, Welsh derbies). Features live in-match tension gauges, unique derby scuffles & turnovers, and dynamic Supporter Trust sentiment that impacts stadium attendance.
9. **Location-Aware Multi-Currency System**: Automatic currency formatting tailored to each club's nation (£ Pound Sterling for UK clubs, € Euro for Ireland/Italy, R Rand for South Africa, $ USD, A$, NZ$). Any amount exceeding 999,000 automatically transitions to concise millions (e.g. $1m, £1.2m).
10. **Custom Exhibition Match Mode**: Freedom to select your own controlled team and pick any AI opponent, set home or away advantage, and play high-intensity derby exhibitions with trophy honours.
11. **Automated Diagnostic Suite**: Comprehensive automated tests verifying positional rules, hybrid mechanics, ratings, substitution compliance, and rivalry tension directly inside the game.
